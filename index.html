Rivers: Federal Office for the Environment, through the federal linked-data service (lindas.admin.ch). Air temperature and precipitation: MeteoSwiss automatic stations (data.geo.admin.ch). The gauges lie below reservoirs and diversions, so they show what each river carries now, not the inflow at a basin site.

Two thirds of the area lies above 2,500 m and mean slope is 30° or more in every band, so flat ground is rare. All 15 ranked sites sit between 1,524 and 2,332 m, below most of the ice.

If the glaciers shrink

usable storage against glacier size

In this scenario every glacier shrinks by the same share. Real retreat removes low-lying ice first, so a basin below a small, low glacier loses its supply sooner than shown. Move the Glacier size control at the top to apply a size to the whole dashboard.

    Method limits. Surface runoff only (D8 routing on the 10 m swissALTI3D mean); subglacial flow and karst are missing. Meltwater is glacier area × the melt rate, with no rain or snowmelt from outside the glaciers. Glacier outlines (2013–2018) are older than the terrain model (2024/25), so forefields that have become ice-free since were not tested. Volumes tend to be slightly too small and dam length is indicative.

    Built by team SwissStainability at the Swiss Hackathon 2026, Lucerne.BasinScope Alps is the upstream triage layer between public Earth-observation data and professional Alpine water-resilience studies. Site names are derived from coordinates and neighbouring glaciers and should be checked on the map. Each site is matched to its Hydro-CH2018 catchment by location; that match is this dashboard's assumption, not part of the terrain analysis.
