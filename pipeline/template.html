Asking for the forecast.

A prototype of trigger conditions, not a warning service. A day is marked Watch when one condition is met and Elevated when thaw and heavy precipitation fall together. The two thresholds are the team's first setting and are not calibrated against past events. Forecast: MeteoSwiss ICON-CH2 through Open-Meteo, at the 15 site locations, refreshed hourly. Official warnings: natural-hazards.ch.

Where a trigger would meet a mapped process

Why this matters here

Blatten, 28 May 2025

The Birch glacier above Blatten in the Lötschental collapsed on 28 May 2025. Ice and rock buried most of the evacuated village and dammed the Lonza. The federal gauge at Blatten has sent no reading since that day.

Three of the 15 ranked sites lie in that valley. Blatten is excluded as a settlement area, and the terrain analysis notes that the ground there has changed fundamentally. Fafleralp and the Jegigletscher forefield lie upstream of it.

  • Shows where federal index maps place a process within 250 m of a site
  • Shows when thaw or heavy precipitation is forecast there
  • States every threshold and lets you move it
  • Predict an event or its size
  • Cover avalanches or glacier collapse
  • Replace a hazard map or an on-site assessment

Avalanches are left out because the federal index map only models avalanches that start in forest, which says nothing above the tree line. The hazard index maps date from 2005 and 2006.

Two thirds of the area lies above 2,500 m and mean slope is 30° or more in every band, so flat ground is rare. All 15 ranked sites sit between 1,524 and 2,332 m, below most of the ice.

Order-of-magnitude estimate, scaled in a straight line from the KWO Trift project (CHF 387 m, 85 Mio m³, 215 GWh of winter energy) and assuming a similar chain of power stations downstream. Investment follows the basin volume, energy follows the water the glaciers can supply. Head, the power chain and costs need a feasibility study.

If the glaciers shrink

usable storage against glacier size

In this scenario every glacier shrinks by the same share. Real retreat removes low-lying ice first, so a basin below a small, low glacier loses its supply sooner than shown. Move the Glacier size control at the top to apply a size to the whole dashboard.

    • A single JSON dataset per region
    • Static JSON API with an OpenAPI description

    • Jungfrau–Aletsch is the first region, with 303 of the about 961 km² of Swiss glacier ice (SGI 2016)

    Method limits. Surface runoff only (D8 routing on the 10 m swissALTI3D mean); subglacial flow and karst are missing. Meltwater is glacier area × the melt rate, with no rain or snowmelt from outside the glaciers. Glacier outlines (2013–2018) are older than the terrain model (2024/25), so forefields that have become ice-free since were not tested. Volumes tend to be slightly too small and dam length is indicative.
